1. About CoachEasier

CoachEasier is software that rugby clubs use to run their teams: squad selection, player availability, training plans, fixtures and team communication. It is currently in beta and clubs are onboarded directly by us. The service is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY TO BE CONFIRMED] of [REGISTERED ADDRESS TO BE CONFIRMED].

2. Scope

This page explains what CoachEasier does with personal information. Most information in CoachEasier is put there by a club — by its coaches, administrators or medical staff — about their own members. Each club decides which people it adds, what it records about them, and who inside the club can see it. 3. Information we collect

Account information. Your first and last name, display name, email address, a securely hashed version of your password, whether your email has been verified, when the account was created and when you last signed in.

Membership and permissions. The club you belong to, your role (for example coach, admin, medical or player), your staff level, the groups and teams you may work with, whether you have medical access, and when you joined or were approved.

Sign-in records. A record of each signed-in session, including when it was created, when it was last seen and when it expires. We store a hashed form of the session token, not the token itself.

Technical records. To protect accounts from abuse we rate-limit sensitive actions and keep a short administrative log of significant events (for example a club being created, an invitation being sent or an email being verified). Those log entries can include the IP address the request came from. The log holds a limited number of the most recent entries and older entries drop off automatically.

Notifications. If you turn on push notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription (an address supplied by your browser vendor plus the keys needed to encrypt messages to it) which we store so we can send you team notifications.

On your device. CoachEasier sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in. The app also keeps its own working state in your browser's local storage so it can show your club's information quickly. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking software of any kind in CoachEasier.

4. Information clubs put into CoachEasier

Coaches and administrators record team information about their members. Depending on what a club chooses to use, that can include: a player's name, playing position, group or team, contact details such as a phone number, a date of birth, an emergency contact, a profile photo, availability answers for training and matches, attendance, squad selections, training plans, fixtures and messages sent through the app. Clubs can also import fixtures or training plans from a spreadsheet.

Clubs decide what to record. We ask clubs to record only what they genuinely need to run their teams.

5. Youth participants

Clubs use CoachEasier for youth teams as well as senior teams. Where a club records information about people under 18, the club is responsible for having the appropriate authority and permissions to do so, and for using that information only for legitimate club and team purposes.

CoachEasier does not market to children and does not invite children to create clubs of their own; accounts are created through a club's own invitation. [TO BE CONFIRMED: the specific consent and guardian arrangements that apply, which depend on the countries clubs operate in, need legal review before public signup.]

6. Injury and medical information

CoachEasier includes a medical area where a club's authorised medical staff can record injury information about players — for example a condition, its severity and status, notes and a timeline of updates. This is sensitive information and it is treated separately inside the product: it is visible only to club users who hold medical access, and only for the groups they are scoped to.

CoachEasier is not a medical device, a healthcare provider or a clinical record system. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice; it stores what a club's own staff choose to write down for team welfare and planning. Clubs should record only what is necessary and should limit medical access to the people who genuinely need it. [TO BE CONFIRMED: the lawful basis and any additional safeguards for this category of information need legal review before public signup.]

7. How information is used

We use the information to provide the service: signing you in, showing your club's squads, availability, training and fixtures, sending team messages and notifications you have asked for, sending necessary account emails (such as verification and password reset), keeping accounts secure, preventing abuse, and supporting you when you contact us.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

8. Who else is involved

We rely on a small number of services to run CoachEasier:

  • Vercel — hosting and the application's server functions.
  • Upstash — the database that stores club and account information.
  • Resend — sending account emails such as verification, password reset and invitations.
  • Browser push services — when notifications are enabled, your own browser vendor's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers the notification to your device.
  • A QR-code image service (api.qrserver.com) — when a coach displays an invitation as a QR code, the invitation link is sent to that service so it can draw the image. If you would rather not use it, share the invitation link directly instead.

Some parts of the interface (a web font service and two code libraries) are delivered from public content networks. Those services see the network request needed to deliver a file; they are not given your club's data.

CoachEasier includes preparatory code for future paid subscriptions using Stripe. No payments are processed today and no payment details are collected. [TO BE CONFIRMED: the final published list of service providers, including the countries their infrastructure operates in.]

9. Sharing

Information is shared inside your club according to the roles and access its administrators configure. Different clubs cannot see each other's information. We do not share personal information with anyone else except the service providers above, or where we are required to by law.

10. Keeping information

Some things expire automatically: sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, single-use invitations expire after 14 days, email-verification links expire after 24 hours, and the administrative log keeps only a limited number of recent entries.

Everything else — accounts, club records, squads, training, messages and medical entries — is kept while it is needed to provide the service to your club. Club administrators can remove members and can reset a club's operational data from within the app, and you can ask us to delete an account or a club's information by emailing support. If you want to know what CoachEasier holds about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email support@coacheasier.com. If the information was entered by a club about you as a member, we may need to involve that club, since it decides what is recorded about its members. 12. Club administrator responsibilities

If you administer a club on CoachEasier, you are responsible for having the authority to add the people you add, for telling your members that their information is held in CoachEasier, for granting access (especially medical access) only to those who need it, and for removing people who leave.

13. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information: passwords are stored only as salted hashes, sessions are stored as hashed tokens and expire, access inside a club is checked on the server for every request, each club's data is stored separately, sensitive actions are rate-limited, and the site is served over HTTPS with a restrictive set of browser security policies. No online service can promise perfect security, and we do not make that promise.
