What this page will cover

CoachEasier is team-management software for rugby clubs. When the policy is published it will set out, in plain language:

  • the account and club information we hold (names, email addresses, roles and the club a person belongs to);
  • the team information coaches enter (squads, availability answers, training plans, fixtures and messages);
  • how injury and medical notes recorded by club medical staff are handled and who can see them;
  • how information about young players is handled;
  • the services we rely on to run CoachEasier (hosting, storage, email and push delivery, and payment processing when subscriptions begin);
  • how long information is kept and how a club or an individual can ask for it to be deleted;
  • who to contact about any of the above.

What is true today

These are current facts about the product, not policy commitments:

  • CoachEasier does not run advertising, and there is no analytics, tracking or marketing software of any kind in the app.
  • The only cookie the app sets is the one that keeps you signed in. Everything else the app stores on your device is the app's own working state.
  • Each club's data is stored separately, and access inside a club follows the roles and groups its administrators configure.
